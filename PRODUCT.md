# Product

## Register
brand

## Users
Editors, directors, assistant editors, production and documentary teams with footage distributed across removable drives, plus creators and families rediscovering personal archives. Based on the adjacent application PRODUCT.md, supplied brief, existing landing page, and product due diligence.

## Product Purpose
Present FrameFinder as a local-first archive discovery and physical-source retrieval workflow. Explain what footage exists, where the original lives, and how to retrieve it. Convert interest into an application for access. Catalogue access never implies disconnected originals can be opened.

## Brand Personality
Precise, restrained, cinematic. Preserve the existing FrameFinder identity and real frontend. Draw atmosphere from an editing room and the material reality of camera files, timecodes, drives and footage.

## Anti-references
Generic SaaS cards, purple AI gradients, decorative neon, fake application dashboards, unsupported exclusivity or pricing claims. No universal offline or privacy compliance promises.

## Design Principles
1. Make footage the visual material and retrieval the story.
2. Use motion to organize chaos into a searchable memory.
3. Preserve source identity and connection status throughout.
4. Demonstrate workflows without asserting benchmarks.
5. Keep the seven requested sections in their exact order.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Semantic sections, keyboard access, visible focus, native modal focus containment, reduced-motion support, strong contrast, and mobile sequences composed vertically. No scroll hijacking.
