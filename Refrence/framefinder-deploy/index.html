09 — FAQ

Quick answers.

The things filmmakers ask before handing an app their archive.

Does FrameFinder upload my footage?+

No. Your media stays on your drives. Keyframes are processed for vectors and tags, but your footage is never stored by the service.

Can I search when a drive is disconnected?+

Yes. The local index keeps searchable keyframes and metadata for every indexed drive, mounted or not.

Do I need a GPU or FFmpeg installed?+

No. AI inference runs on the FrameFinder service, and FFmpeg ships with the app.

Which Macs are supported?+

macOS 13 Ventura or later, on both Apple Silicon and Intel. Apple Silicon with 16 GB RAM is recommended.
