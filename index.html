03 / Local-first by design

Terabytes
shouldn’t need
to travel.

No need to migrate your full-resolution archive into a cloud media library. Your source footage stays under your control.

From a documentary field kit to a MENA production studio, your archive shouldn’t depend on master-file upload speed.

Connected analysis can process extracted keyframes remotely. Catalogue data stays on your Mac; this is a local-first workflow, not a claim that every feature works without internet.
